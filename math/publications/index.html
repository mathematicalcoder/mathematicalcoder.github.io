An introduction to matroid theory
Version exp-20260624-a60dbdv1

Written for Euler Circle Independent Research and Paper Writing 2025.

Abstract. In this paper, we explore matroids, which generalize properties of linear independence of vectors and cycles in graphs. We investigate properties and operations on matroids, then we look at how we can represent matroids using matrices.
